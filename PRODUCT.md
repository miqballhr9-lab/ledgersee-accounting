# workspace

**Platform:** Responsive web — Next.js, React, Tailwind. shadcn/ui and lucide are installed and available; the direction decides whether they fit or whether this surface needs its own vocabulary.
**Task mode:** Operate (pass as `--mode operate` to any script)

## What this is

Build a functional accounting web application inspired by Zoho Books, with a left sidebar navigation and a clean, professional business-software look. The UI should be in English (with Roman Urdu labels where natural if desired). Include these working modules, each with real create/read/edit/delete and stored data (persist in the browser or a backend): 1. Dashboard — KPI cards (total income, total expenses, net profit, receivables, payables) and a simple income-vs-expense chart. 2. Customers — list, add, edit, delete customers; contact details, opening balance. 3. Vendors — same for suppliers. 4. Items / Products & Services — name, SKU, rate, unit, tax, stock quantity. 5. Invoices — create invoices with line items, tax, discount, due date; statuses (Draft, Sent, Paid, Overdue, Partially Paid); record payments; convert from an estimate. 6. Estimates / Quotes — create and convert to invoice. 7. Sales Orders — create and convert to invoice. 8. Credit Notes / Refunds — apply against invoices. 9. Recurring Invoices — simple recurring invoice setup. 10. Bills (vendor bills) — record vendor bills, mark paid, and Expense tracking with attachments/categories. 11. Purchase Orders — create and convert to bill. 12. Banking — add bank/cash accounts, record deposits and withdrawals, a simple "reconcile/match transaction" view, and bank rules/categorization. 13. Chart of Accounts — asset/liability/income/expense/equity account types, manual journals (double-entry). 14. Taxes — configurable tax rates (e.g. GST/VAT with CGST/SGST breakdown), tax summary. 15. Reports — Profit & Loss, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow, Trial Balance, Sales by Customer, Expenses by Category, Accounts Receivable/Payable ageing, with date-range filters and export to CSV. 16. Multi-currency — at least a currency setting and exchange rate field on invoices. 17. Users & Roles — add team members with roles (Admin, Accountant, Sales, Viewer) and a permissions switch. 18. Settings — company profile (name, logo, address, currency, fiscal year), invoice numbering, and email templates. Also include: search and filter on every list, pagination, and a simple login/signup screen so data is per-account. Make it responsive. This is a demo/working app, not a production system — but every listed module must actually function end to end, not be a dead placeholder.
