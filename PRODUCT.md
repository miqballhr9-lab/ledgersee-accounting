# MIKLEDGER

**Platform:** Responsive web — Next.js, React, Tailwind. shadcn/ui and lucide are installed and available; the direction decides whether they fit or whether this surface needs its own vocabulary.
**Task mode:** Operate (pass as `--mode operate` to any script)

## What this is

MIKLEDGER is a small-business bookkeeping and payroll workspace for customers, sales documents, purchases, banking, and financial reports.

## What it enables

Give owners a clear, account-specific place to manage routine bookkeeping and follow outstanding money.

## Primary user

Founders, freelancers, and small operators who need practical finance workflows without a complex setup.

## What exists today

Email and password sign-up/sign-in with records isolated to the signed-in account. - Read-only sample workspace preview for the dashboard, invoices, and expenses; preview records are not saved. - Dashboard KPIs and monthly income-versus-expense chart based on saved records. - CRUD workspaces for customers, vendors, items, invoices, estimates, sales orders, credit notes, recurring invoices, bills, expenses, purchase orders, banking, accounts, journals, taxes, team roles, and company settings. - Purchase orders follow an RFQ workflow: email vendors, confirm orders, receive products into linked item stock, cancel eligible orders, then create one bill after receipt. - Inventory workspace tracks product on-hand quantities, reorder points, unit-cost valuation, linked purchase receipts, manual deliveries, and physical counts; downloadable Excel count sheets can reconcile up to 500 products in one atomic save, with each changed quantity recorded in stock-movement history. - Bills have a dedicated payables workspace with outstanding and overdue totals, vendor/date/category visibility, status and category filters, CSV export, and direct edit, delete, and mark-paid actions. - Expense entry captures payee, payment account/date/method, reference number, memo, receipt, and multiple Chart of Accounts category-description-amount lines; line totals feed expense lists, category reports, and budget actuals. - Suppliers have a QuickBooks-style directory with display names, search across contact, address, and tax details, open/overdue balance filters, payable summaries, CSV export, and add/edit/delete actions; profiles include contact and structured address details, tax and account IDs, payment terms, notes, attachments, opening balances, and a default expense category prefilled on new bills. - Invoice email delivery and payment tracking; estimates and sales orders convert to invoices; purchase orders convert to bills; recurring schedules generate invoices; credit notes apply to invoices. - Bank reconciliation by statement date and ending balance, with transaction clearing, balanced-close validation, and per-account history. - Search, category/status filters, pagination, receipt/logo uploads, searchable categorized financial reports with date filters and CSV export, plus saved budgets with Sales/Expenses/Payroll categories, matching account and transaction heads, Gross Salary actuals by payroll month, expected/actual variance, source-record drill-down, custom date ranges, copy, email sending, and category-aware CSV template import/export. - Company settings provide a searchable choice of active global currencies; new invoices, sales documents, and bank accounts inherit the selected default. - Invoice, estimate, sales order, credit note, and recurring invoice forms let owners choose from their saved customers, showing company or email details while keeping the customer name on the record. - Invoice, estimate, sales order, and credit note forms let owners choose saved items and services, create a catalog item directly from an empty line, add or remove rows, edit per-line descriptions, adjust quantities and rates, and preview currency-aware tax, discount, and total calculations. - Items & services support Excel template downloads and bulk imports of up to 500 products or services, with required-field, numeric, and duplicate-SKU checks. - Invoices, estimates, sales orders, and credit notes support saved items and services, PDF/image/CSV attachments, draft copies, public share links, print/PDF views, and delivery by email or a prepared WhatsApp/SMS message. Invoices and bills can use a selected Cash, Bank, or Credit card account from the Chart of Accounts, or remain unpaid on credit; invoices also support due-on-receipt or 15/30/60-day terms and payment tracking. - Bill and purchase-order forms let owners choose from saved vendors, showing company or email details while keeping the vendor name on the record. - Manual journals support multiple debit and credit lines, account choices from Chart of Accounts, balanced totals, and CSV line import; the Trial Balance includes these entries and remains compatible with older journals. - Chart of Accounts has searchable and type-filtered account rows, grouped account types with matching detail choices, optional account numbers, subaccounts, dated opening balances with a balance-sheet preview, and account locking; accounts used by journal entries cannot be deleted. - A back arrow in the workspace header returns to the previous page, with a dashboard fallback for directly opened sections. - Monthly payroll records are saved per account with employee ID, email, joining date, CNIC, salary increments, attendance, allowances, Pakistan Tax Year 2027 salary-tax estimates, deductions, adjustments, descriptions, a Chart of Accounts payout account, and final payable salary; entries support search, edit, delete, next-month copy, printable salary slips, email delivery, custom date ranges, and CSV template import/export that updates matching employee/month rows.
